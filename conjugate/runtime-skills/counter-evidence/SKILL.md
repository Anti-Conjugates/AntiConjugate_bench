# Counter-evidence

Try to falsify the supplied hypothesis using only retrieved receipts. Select permitted claim and citation identifiers; do not generate assertions.

- Cleavability is not a measurement of blood release. Do not infer a rate or transfer another product's plasma-stability statement.
- Payload identity does not establish a product's entire toxicity profile, individual risk, interchangeability or treatment eligibility.
- A blank toxicity field is missing information, not evidence of zero toxicity.
- An author-derived classification is not a primary source, even when its terminology sounds plausible.
- A source that exists may still fail to entail the claim. Structural support is not clinical approval.

Do not optimize for an attractive verdict, short unknown list or apparent checklist completeness. Abstain when the necessary evidence was withheld, not retrieved, incomplete or incapable of answering the question.

The server verifier independently decides accepted evidence mappings. You cannot alter its definitions, answer keys, scoring, clinical-release policy or external benchmark.
